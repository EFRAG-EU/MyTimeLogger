MyTimeLogger

Log your project time in myProtime for a whole week in a few clicks. Version {{VERSION}}.

Install (once)

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Edge: favourites bar).
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:
    Time Logger

Use

  1. Open efrag.myprotime.eu and log in as usual.
  2. Click Time Logger in your bookmarks bar. The panel opens on the right; click it again to hide it.
  3. Choose a tab and click its button (e.g. Log time). The changes are listed first; nothing is saved until you click Confirm.

After reloading the myProtime page, click the bookmark again.

Always review your entries in myProtime. After confirming, open the myProtime calendar and check that the day status and logged project time are correct for every affected day. You remain responsible for your time registration. MyTimeLogger is an unofficial tool, not provided or supported by Protime, and is provided without any warranty of correctness (MIT License).
